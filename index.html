About Us

We bring students, makers and mentors together to build creative solutions through focused hackathons and collaborative learning.

Our Mission

To empower young innovators with hands-on events, mentorship, and resources that turn ideas into working prototypes and real-world impact.

  • Encourage practical learning
  • Support cross-disciplinary teams
  • Connect students with mentors and resources

What We Do

We organize hackathons, workshops, and collaborative sessions that focus on rapid prototyping, teamwork, and skill-building.

Typical outcomes: working demos, learning portfolios, new friendships, and prizes.
